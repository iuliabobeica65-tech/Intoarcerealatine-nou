INDIVIDUAL PROGRAMME · FOUR MONTHS

From recurring situations towards more conscious choices.

It upsets you when others decide for you. But when you have a decision to make, you ask for their approval before choosing. You tried being more decisive, yet a single opinion can make you change your mind. It hurts that standing by your own choice is difficult. In this individual journey, we begin with your specific situation, work with perceptions influencing your reactions and build choices closer to what you want.

What do we work with during the four months?

Are you tired of repeating the same situations in relationships? We begin with what hurts, look for the conclusions about yourself emerging in your story and work with how they influence your choices.

How does the journey unfold?

We work with your inner dialogue and perceptions about yourself and life, formed in important past experiences. We use the validated balancing questions and check what you notice differently in how you see and choose. Conscious creation is present from the beginning: you notice what you want, what matters to you and which choices you begin building in your life.

The programme lasts four months. The pace of meetings, availability and participation conditions are clarified personally. I do not promise identical results for every participant.

Begin with the situation you want to change

Tell me what repeats in your life and what you want to be different. We discuss the individual programme, availability and how we can work together.
