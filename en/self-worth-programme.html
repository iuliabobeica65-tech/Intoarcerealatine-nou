What does it cost you? You constantly postpone your wishes. What might you have understood? That only what is left over is all right for you. See whether this is your story too. A SITUATION YOU MAY RECOGNISE

‘I worked so hard. And it still goes unseen.’

After many days of taking care that things turn out well, you receive very little recognition in the end. Perhaps you already spoke about how much having your work appreciated matters to you. Yet you leave with the same heaviness.

It hurts that people seem to pass over the time and energy you invested.

This is an illustrative situation. It does not mean every woman who works hard experiences the same pain, or that we know the cause before hearing her story.

ABOUT THE PROGRAMME

How we work with guilt, shame and how you see yourself

01 · We begin with you

The situation you are experiencing now

We begin with a specific situation in which you felt guilt or shame and explore what you told yourself about yourself then.

02 · Echilibrarea rădăcinilor

Exploring the polarised root

We explore the interpretation formed around an experience relevant to you and how you view it today. Constellations may be used according to context.

03 · Echilibrarea rădăcinilor

Balancing perceptions

We work with the specific, observable action. The balancing questions come exclusively from the universal laws, rather than questions or exercises created for this programme.

04 · In the same process

Checking and conscious creation

We check what changed in the interpretation and how you experience the situation. Conscious creation also takes place during balancing.

These are components of the way of working, not four fixed stages assigned one per week. We choose the theme and pace according to your situation.

CLARITY THROUGH BALANCING PERCEPTIONS

Why I use Echilibrarea rădăcinilor

In my method, the root we explore is polarisation: the interpretation the mind gave an event. The universal laws I use are Duality, Reflection, Transformation, Synchronicity, Order, Fractals and Eristic Escalation. In balancing, we work with the observable action through questions from the universal laws, then check whether the interpretation changed.

Depending on the situation explored, we may also use systemic constellations to notice other perspectives on relationships and experiences important to you.

In my explanation, the sense of worthiness relates to the subconscious mind, where emotions, beliefs and patterns accumulate throughout life. Interpreting some experiences may be accompanied by shame, guilt or feeling indebted, but also pride, superiority or entitlement. We explore these polarities for a more balanced self-image. The sense of worthiness is not a medical score or a scientific measure of your value. The programme does not promise all difficulties disappear in four weeks.

What is useful to know beforehand

Do I need to know the cause of my problem already?

We begin with the present situation and what you share. We discover together what matters to you.

Is this programme only about money?

No. We can work with situations around recognition, asking, receiving, giving, appreciation and worthiness, beginning with your experience.

How long does it last and how many meetings are included?

The programme lasts four weeks with four individual meetings, usually one per week. Details about each meeting's duration, price and availability are confirmed before you begin.

Does it replace psychotherapy or treatment?

No. It is a personal development programme, not medical treatment or a psychotherapeutic intervention.
