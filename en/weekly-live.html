Free weekly live meeting

Every Monday at 7:00 PM, Romania time, on Jitsi. A space to explore everyday situations, recurring patterns, personal values and new perspectives through questions and conversation.

What happens during the meeting?

We start with real-life situations and questions. You may participate by listening; you do not have to share personal details. Voluntary demonstrations may sometimes take place.
