A SITUATION YOU MAY RECOGNISE

You say ‘it is nothing’. But afterwards, it hurts that you were not heard.

You wanted to talk with someone close about something bothering you. You postponed it so an argument would not begin. That evening you feel alone with the problem, and the other person may not even know it exists.

What does it cost you? Tension remains and you begin believing your wishes do not matter. What might you have learned? Perhaps you experienced situations where expressing an opinion drew reproaches. Perhaps you came to believe you must stay silent to keep the peace. See whether you recognise anything from your own experience.

At the LIVE, we begin with painful situations. We listen to what you experience and look at things from several angles together.

A MEETING EVERY MONDAY

A place for questions.
And for other perspectives.

In the LIVE, we begin with real, painful situations and the questions they raise: why do they repeat, what do you tell yourself about yourself and what do you want to be different? Through explanations, discussion and questions, you can get to know how I work better.

There may also be voluntary demonstrations. You can participate simply by listening without speaking about your personal life.

PERHAPS YOU ARE WONDERING

A few answers,
before you come.

Do I have to talk about myself?

No. You can listen without discussing your personal life. Involvement in a demonstration is voluntary.

Can I participate without experience?

Yes. You do not need to know the method before the meeting. Explanations and questions are a way to become familiar with how I work.

Are there demonstrations?

There may be voluntary demonstrations. Attending the LIVE does not oblige you to participate in one and does not mean a complete individual process.

What if I cannot attend one Monday?

Meetings take place weekly, on Mondays between 19:00 and 20:00. You can request details for a future meeting.

Do I use constellations in my method?

Yes. According to context, systemic constellations help me explore the root of a polarised perception. Not every meeting includes a constellation; participating in a group representation requires the person's consent.

What does the Echilibrarea rădăcinilor programme involve?

It is an individual process adapted to your situation and goals. We begin with what you experience now, explore perceptions and relevant situations, check changes and clarify actions you want to take. We do not promise to eliminate every pattern within a fixed time.

Is it therapy?

The LIVE is an exploration and personal development meeting. It is not psychotherapy and does not replace medical or psychological support you need.

How do I request information about individual work?

You can ask for details by private message without an obligation to begin working together. You can use the ‘I would like to learn more’ button to open WhatsApp with a prepared message.
