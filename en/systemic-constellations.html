What can we explore?

Questions about family relationships, recurring interpersonal situations and your sense of belonging can be explored. We do not assume that a family history is the cause of a current difficulty.

A separate offering

Systemic constellations are offered separately from the Root Balancing Method. They are not a required step and do not replace mental-health assessment or treatment.

Ask about an online session

Online sessions may be discussed in English or Romanian. Send a message to ask about the format, availability and whether it suits your needs.
