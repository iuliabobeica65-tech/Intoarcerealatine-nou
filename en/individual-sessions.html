You say you can manage alone. But does it hurt when nobody helps?

Someone offers to help and you reply that you can manage. That evening, after doing everything alone, it hurts that nobody insisted. You tried asking for help, yet the situation returns. Beyond everything you do, the pain of needing support and feeling alone with it remains.

When you know what you want to change, but react the same way

You intended to say more clearly what you want, ask for help and make room for yourself among your own priorities. Yet in certain moments, you return to the same response. Dissatisfaction remains, even though you tried doing things differently.

In an individual session, we go beyond the immediate reaction. We identify what you came to believe about yourself and work with the root of the problem you want to change.

Where do we begin?

You tell me about the situation that hurts: what happened, what you tried and what you want to change. Together, we look for the moment you reached a particular conclusion about yourself and work with perceptions influencing your choices.

Conscious creation is present from the beginning, through how you notice your perceptions, values and choices. Family and systemic constellations are a distinct way of working, used according to context.

What do you aim for in this process?

Changing how you respond to painful situations, balancing your perceptions and building choices closer to what you want. The work adapts to your situation and does not replace psychological or medical care when needed.
