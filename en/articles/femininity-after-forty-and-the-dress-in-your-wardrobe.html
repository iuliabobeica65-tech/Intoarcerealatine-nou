The Dress You Loved Stayed in the Wardrobe. What Has Changed for You?

You open the wardrobe and see the dress you used to enjoy wearing. You take it off the hanger, but put it back before trying it on. You are getting ready to go out and feel that nothing you used to like represents you anymore.

When old clothes no longer tell the same story

Perhaps your body has changed. Perhaps your tastes are different. Perhaps you simply no longer want to dress as you did ten years ago. None of these changes says anything about your worth or how feminine you are.

You tried different cuts, bought a few clothes or looked for style ideas. You liked some, but still find it difficult to choose something in which you feel yourself. It may hurt to feel you have grown distant from a part of yourself you valued.

What do you want now, rather than ten years ago?

What did you like about that dress? How did you feel wearing it? What no longer fits today: the fabric, cut, occasion or how you see yourself? What have you already tried, and what remained unchanged?

There is no single definition of femininity, or an age at which you should look a certain way. You can keep what you like from the past and choose something else for the present.

How we explore in Root Balancing

We begin with the specific situation and what you understood about yourself. We explore perceptions through balancing questions, without assuming a cause. Conscious creation is present from the beginning, including as you clarify how you want to express yourself today.
