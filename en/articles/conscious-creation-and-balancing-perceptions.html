Conscious Creation: How You Begin to Build a Different Reality

2026-10-29 · Iulia Bobeica

You want to begin a project. You have prepared, have ideas and know what to do. But before taking the first step, you begin thinking: ‘What if others criticise me? What if I make a mistake? What if they say I am not good enough?’ And you postpone again.

You promise yourself you will have courage next time. Except next time the same thing happens.

Why does a pattern repeat?

How you think about yourself and others is influenced by your experiences and the conclusions you drew then. At the time, those conclusions may have helped you adapt. Today, however, they may no longer help you make the choices you want.

In my method, Echilibrarea rădăcinilor, we begin with the problem that hurts now. We explore what actually happened, how you came to think about yourself that way and which experiences are relevant. There may be several root moments, not necessarily only one.

Clarity through balancing perceptions

In Echilibrarea rădăcinilor, we explore both sides of the experience using balancing questions from the universal laws. We work on the observable action and then check what changes in your interpretation.

I do not tell you what you experienced or what you should have understood. The answers are yours.

What does conscious creation mean to me?

Conscious creation is present from the beginning of the process, rather than appearing only after exploring the past. From the first moment you notice how you react and what you choose, you begin looking more closely at how you participate in your own life.

We do not work with the past to remain there. I am interested in what becomes possible now when you begin seeing a situation differently.

If you previously stayed silent for fear of upsetting people, you may begin expressing your point of view. If you previously waited for everyone's approval, you may begin making your own decisions.

This is what I call conscious creation: as you change your perceptions, your way of thinking and how you act, you begin consciously building a different experience of your life. It is not about controlling what others do or guaranteed results.

The Law of Fractals and everyday life

I see the Law of Fractals as a reference point for noticing a pattern that may arise in several contexts. You may seek approval in a relationship and recognise the same response when making a decision at work. If you begin changing a way of reacting, you may discover other possibilities in several situations. We check real changes, however, without assuming all problems automatically disappear.
