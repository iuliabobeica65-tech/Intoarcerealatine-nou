When a Relationship Breaks Down, What Happens to Your Place in the Family?

You have discovered that your partner has been unfaithful. There are conversations, arguments and questions about what to do next. If you have children, you are also concerned about what their lives will look like. This is not only about the relationship. It is also about what you have come to believe about yourself.

This is an illustrative example, not a client's story. There is no identical answer for every woman, nor an explanation I could give before knowing her experience.

Infidelity, divorce, separation: what is there to explore?

Infidelity can bring pain, shame, anger, mistrust and questions about your own worth. Someone's infidelity does not prove that their partner ‘was not good enough’. We do not assign responsibility for someone else's choice to the woman who was hurt.

A divorce or separation can change family arrangements, relationships with parents, responsibilities and children's lives. Sometimes arguments continue, or you feel you must choose between taking care of yourself and pleasing others.

In family and systemic constellation meetings, we can symbolically explore how you see relationships and your place within them. I do not use this representation to decide who is to blame, predict what a former partner will do or tell a child how they should feel.

When a child is caught in the middle

Think of a situation in which adults argue and a child tries to reconcile them, or to be so well behaved that there will be no more conflict. As an adult, that person may recognise the same need to mediate: ‘If I do not make peace, everyone gets upset.’

I do not automatically assume the present comes from that scene. We explore what the person remembers and the conclusions they formed. Sometimes several experiences may be relevant, rather than just one.

A family constellations workshop in Bacău or online

A family constellations workshop is a space for exploration through symbolic representation. Depending on the format, you can take part with a personal theme or as a representative. Participants' consent and boundaries matter.

If the exploration brings a polarised perception into focus, in my method I work with the balancing questions from the universal laws, on the specific action, and we then check what has changed in the interpretation. Conscious creation also means noticing, in the present, which choice is yours to make.

Constellations are not couples psychotherapy and do not guarantee reconciliation, divorce, a change in a partner's behaviour or the resolution of a family conflict. In situations of abuse or violence, safety and specialist help take priority.
