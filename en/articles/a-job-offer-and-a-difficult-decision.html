You Have Received a Job Offer. How Do You Choose Without Losing Your Peace?

Between two options, without peace

You have received a job offer. On paper, it looks interesting. But salary is not all that matters: the schedule, commute, family time and expenses change too.

You asked for details. You spoke with people you trust. You made calculations. Yet days pass without your feeling clear enough.

At bedtime, the same question returns: ‘What if I choose and then regret it?’

It is not only the decision that tires you, but having no peace even when you try to rest.

Not all delayed decisions have the same explanation. And some genuinely need time and information, rather than encouragement to ‘be brave’.

You have analysed every option. What remains unresolved?

Perhaps you made a list of advantages and disadvantages. You asked about the commute, calculated expenses and discussed things with your family. What information is still missing? And which question returns even after you have practical answers?

When you imagine accepting the offer, what unsettles you? And when you imagine refusing? What do you believe you would lose in each option, and what might you gain? Not to push you towards either, but to notice what matters to you.

Perhaps you have postponed other decisions. Or perhaps this is one of the few situations in which you cannot decide. It would not be fair to call it a pattern without knowing your story. The question remains: what have you tried already, and what has not helped you reach the clarity you seek?

You can sit with the questions for a little while

You do not need to reach an explanation or solution immediately. In Root Balancing, I begin with the present situation, what you experienced and your conclusions, without assuming the cause.
