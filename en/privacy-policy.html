Privacy policy

How data is used when you visit the page or write to me.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

1. Who manages the page

The Echilibrarea rădăcinilor page is managed by Bobeica Iulia Alexandra, Romania. For questions about personal data or requests for access, correction or deletion, you can write to me at Iuliabobeica65@gmail.com.

This page presents personal development meetings and ways to get in touch. It has no registration form, payment system or user accounts.

2. What data may be processed

  • When you write to me: your name or profile identifier, phone number or email address, and the information you choose to share in WhatsApp, Messenger or email messages.
  • When you access the website: hosting provider Cloudflare may process technical data about HTTP requests, such as IP address, browser type, access date and time, to deliver and protect the website.

I do not request medical data or other sensitive information through this page. Please avoid sending sensitive information in unsolicited messages.

3. Purposes and legal bases for processing

  • Responding to requests and providing meeting information: steps requested before a possible collaboration (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), where applicable; for general questions, the legitimate interest in responding to received messages (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Website operation and security: the legitimate interest in providing a functioning page and preventing abuse (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), within applicable limits.
  • Legal obligations: only where compliance with a legal obligation is necessary (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

There is no newsletter, advertising profiling or automated decision-making through this page.

4. Who may receive the data

Third-party services are used for page operation and communication: Cloudflare for website hosting, Google for email correspondence and Meta for WhatsApp and Messenger, depending on the channel you choose. Each platform may also process data under its own policies.

See also the providers' information: Cloudflare, Google, WhatsApp and Meta.

Depending on the service used, some processing may involve providers or infrastructure outside the European Economic Area. For safeguards and transfer mechanisms applicable to the relevant service, consult the providers' policies above or request information by email.

5. How long data is retained

Received messages are retained for as long as necessary to handle the request and afterwards only as needed to continue a requested discussion, comply with legal obligations or protect rights. Messages no longer needed may be deleted. Cloudflare's retention of technical data is also determined by the operation of the hosting service.

6. Your rights

Under the conditions set out in the GDPR, you may request access to your data, rectification or deletion, restriction of processing, portability where applicable and objection to processing based on legitimate interests. If processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time for the future.

To exercise your rights: Iuliabobeica65@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP).

7. External links and cookies

WhatsApp and Messenger buttons open external services only when you use them. This website version does not embed forms or advertising pixels. Google Analytics 4 loads only after analytics consent. Cookie details are available in the Cookie policy.
