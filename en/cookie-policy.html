Which technologies are used in this version of the page.

Last technical review: 19 October 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files a website or service can store in your browser. Other storage or identification technologies fall under similar rules.

2. What does the Root Balancing page use?

The website is built mainly from static HTML pages. Presentation pages contain local JavaScript for navigating the image gallery and loading editable texts and events from JSON files. These features do not in themselves use analytics or marketing cookies and do not include a registration form. Google Analytics 4 is activated only after explicit consent. This integration does not install Meta Pixel. Separately, the hosting provider may process technical requests and use its own technologies; this must be checked in the browser at the website's final address.

3. Is a consent banner necessary?

Google Analytics 4 uses optional analytics cookies only after explicit acceptance. The absence of these services from the code does not in itself prove the hosting does not use cookies. Before final publication, actual cookies and network requests must be checked. If we identify non-essential technologies requiring consent, they will not be activated before a choice is expressed, and the policy and consent mechanism will be adapted. We do not display a decorative banner without a real function.

Cookies strictly necessary to transmit communications or provide an expressly requested service may fall within statutory exceptions.

4. Links to other services

Using WhatsApp or Messenger buttons takes you away from this page into a third-party service that may use its own technologies and rules: WhatsApp and Meta.

5. How can you control cookies?

You can view and delete cookies in your browser settings, usually under ‘Privacy → Cookies and site data’. Disabling strictly necessary cookies on other websites may affect how they function.
