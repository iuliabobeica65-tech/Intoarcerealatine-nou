Do you say yes although you wanted to say no?

You made a plan for yourself. Someone asks for help and you immediately give it up. Later, you feel upset that your time does not matter. The cost: tiredness and dissatisfaction. What you may have learned: that refusing means disappointing people, and their disappointment is your responsibility. This is not an imposed explanation; see whether you recognise yourself. In the programme, we begin with your specific situations and work with perceptions, without promises of guaranteed results.

DO YOU RECOGNISE THIS SITUATION?

You take care of everyone. But when you choose for yourself, guilt arises.

You say yes so as not to disappoint. You think ten times before setting a boundary. You want change, but return to the same responses. Perhaps you no longer even know when you began believing your needs come last.

We begin with the situation that hurts now, what you tell yourself about yourself and the change you want.

Sometimes, at the root of a difficulty, we discover a fear: of rejection, abandonment, disappointing others or losing something important. We do not decide it in advance; we identify it together and explore the perceptions and conclusions about yourself that support it.

HOW WE WORK TOGETHER

We work actively with situations participants bring, through questions and systemic constellations.

We meet online for six weeks, approximately two hours per meeting. Each week, we work with group systemic constellations and explore polarised perceptions through Echilibrarea rădăcinilor.

Participation in personal work is voluntary; a complete individual constellation for every participant at every meeting is not promised.

The day and time are set by mutual agreement after enrolment, so the whole group can attend. We will most likely meet in the evening or on Saturdays. The first group finishes by the end of the year.

We will also have a private group for communication between meetings. This is a personal development programme and does not replace psychotherapy or medical care.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is useful to know

Will there be constellations every week?

Yes. Every meeting includes group systemic constellation work, integrated with exploring and balancing perceptions through Echilibrarea rădăcinilor.

Do I have to share something personal in front of the group?

No. You choose how much and what you want to share. Confidentiality and respect for personal boundaries are essential.

How do we know which problem to work on?

We begin with the present situation that hurts, rather than assumptions about the past or family.

When do meetings take place?

We agree together after enrolment, depending on the group's availability. Likely options are evenings or Saturdays.
