ECHILIBRAREA RĂDĂCINILOR

Echilibrarea rădăcinilor

Discover the root of the patterns holding you back and begin consciously building a new reality.

You have tried to change what hurts. You have talked, explained and asked for help. Yet in certain situations, you find yourself back at the same point.

Behind recurring reactions, we explore experiences, perceptions and conclusions about yourself, with care and gentleness.

WHEN YOU RECOGNISE YOURSELF IN WHAT YOU ARE EXPERIENCING

You know what you want. But the same pain returns.

You are tired of being strong. Of caring for everyone, carrying responsibilities and promising yourself you will look after yourself too when you have time. You have asked for support and explained how tired you are, but find yourself feeling alone with everything again.

You want love, but closeness comes with fear of being left. You want to trust, yet after a betrayal you find yourself checking and expecting to be disappointed again.

You want to make your own decisions, but seek others' approval. When they decide for you, it hurts that what you want does not matter. You know you need rest, but when you try choosing yourself, guilt arises.

‘Why, if I know what I want, do I keep doing things that hurt me?’

Some pain goes much deeper

Discover the experiences you recognise. Open each theme at your own pace.

Love, relationships and betrayal

You have experienced infidelity and lost trust. You feel rejected or unseen in a relationship. After divorce or separation, you face the feeling that you have failed. You fear abandonment and find leaving hurtful relationships difficult.

You begin wondering why you are not chosen, appreciated or loved as you want to be.

Losses and deeply painful experiences

You lost someone dear and the world kept moving while you were still trying to understand what happened. You experienced the loss of a child, a miscarriage or an abortion, and carry pain, questions or guilt that are difficult to talk about.

You face infertility, repeated hopes and disappointments. Each loss has its own story and deserves to be met with respect and gentleness.

Family, belonging and family history

You have a difficult relationship with your mother or father. There are conflicts, separations, secrets, excluded people or painful events in your family that were rarely discussed. You feel responsible for everyone's peace and try to reconcile, help and repair.

You face the feeling that you cannot find your place or must always prove something to be accepted. In systemic exploration, we also address adoption, abuse, losses, addictions and transgenerational experiences.

Guilt, shame and personal worth

You compare yourself with others and see them as better than you. You receive a compliment and downplay it. You have achievements but find enjoying them difficult. You want more, but fear you do not deserve it.

You have read, learned and tried to change something, yet in important situations the same reactions return: fear of judgement, powerlessness, submission, lack of confidence or feeling you are not enough.

WHEN PAIN HAS A NAME

Five emotional wounds, five different experiences

Sometimes you speak of the abandonment, rejection, betrayal, humiliation or injustice wound. These are terms used in personal development, not diagnoses. What matters is what you actually experienced and what you came to believe about yourself.

BEYOND SYMPTOMS

What have you come to believe about yourself?

After betrayal, rejection or loss, some people begin seeing themselves through conclusions such as ‘I am not enough’, ‘I do not deserve to be loved’ or ‘I must manage alone’.

Over time, such perceptions may appear in relationships, choices and repeating patterns. We explore what actually happened, the meaning you gave the experience and how you recognise it today.

HOW WE WORK

An in-depth process, at your pace

01

We explore the root of the pattern

We begin with what you experience today and discover the experiences in which you formed conclusions about yourself. We notice how they appear in the present.

02

We balance and integrate

We work with balancing questions based on the universal laws: duality, reflection and synchronicity. Depending on the context, I also use family and systemic constellations as a distinct way of exploring.

03

We consciously build a new reality

As we work with perceptions, you clarify your values, wishes and choices. Conscious creation accompanies the whole process; it is not only a final stage.

FAMILY AND SYSTEMIC CONSTELLATIONS

We also explore deep experiences from family history

In constellations, themes may emerge such as the loss of a child, abortion, abuse, infidelity, exclusion of family members, adoption, secrets, grief or transgenerational experiences.

Constellations offer perspectives for exploring relationships, belonging and family dynamics. What emerges is checked together with the person in relation to their own experience, without treating interpretations as proven causes.

Depending on the context, we continue through honouring and integration in systemic work and through balancing perceptions with the universal laws.

We work in depth, with care, respect and gentleness towards painful experiences.

You actively participate in building a different story about yourself. Working with past experiences and conscious creation meet in your choices today.

A root is not always the first thing we discover.

We begin with what hurts today. But as we explore, we may discover the present problem relates to several perceptions and conclusions about yourself.

A question can open another question. An experience can bring another moment in life into focus. A conclusion can appear in relationships, in how you express your needs or in the choices you make. We do not assume they all have the same origin.

Through questions from the universal laws, we also explore the opposite, without denying what you experienced or imposing another interpretation. We balance the observable action and then check the meanings.

Identifying a root does not mean only finding an explanation. We look for clarity you recognise and changes you notice in daily life. If the difficulty returns, we continue exploring.
