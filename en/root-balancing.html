Starting with the present

We begin with a situation that matters to you: what happened, how you responded, and what you concluded about yourself. Together we explore whether a familiar interpretation leaves out other perspectives.

Exploring the roots

Relevant earlier experiences may be explored without assuming that one event explains your whole life. Questions informed by the principles of duality, reflection and synchronicity can be used as perspectives for reflection, not as scientifically established laws of nature.

Returning to your choices

The intention is to examine your perception, clarify what matters to you and consider actions you want to test in daily life. Outcomes are individual and are not guaranteed.
