WORK WITH ME

Do you find yourself in the same situations, even though you have tried to change something?

You tell yourself that this time you will take better care of yourself. That you will say what you want. That you will no longer immediately agree to everything asked of you. But a familiar situation arises and you catch yourself reacting in the same way.

You have tried to be more decisive, to set boundaries, to think positively. And yet, at certain moments, you return to the same choices.

It is not enough to decide to do things differently. It is worth discovering what brings you back to the same reactions.

Echilibrarea rădăcinilor — beyond what is visible on the surface

We start with the specific situation that hurts now. We explore what you have experienced, what you have come to understand about yourself, and how these perceptions influence your choices.

We use balancing questions to look at experiences from different perspectives. Conscious creation is present from the very beginning of the process.

The aim is to understand your reactions, clarify your choices and begin consciously building what you want in your life.

The questions are mine. The answers are yours.

In individual work, we begin with the situation that concerns you now. We explore what you perceived, what you came to understand about yourself, and where you have encountered the same conclusions before.

Sometimes one answer opens several directions. I do not automatically stop at the first explanation, and I do not decide for you what your experiences mean.

We also explore opposite perspectives through questions based on the universal laws, then check how you see the experience and what changes in your reactions and choices. Conscious creation is present throughout our work.

Between meetings, we pay attention to what you notice in everyday life. If the difficulty persists, we continue exploring, without assuming there is only one root.
