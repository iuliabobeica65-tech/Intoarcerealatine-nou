WORK WITH ME

Do you find yourself in the same situations, although you tried changing something?

You tell yourself you will take more care of yourself this time. That you will say what you want. That you will no longer immediately accept everything asked of you. But a familiar situation arises and you find yourself reacting the same way.

You have tried being more decisive, setting boundaries, thinking positively. Yet in certain moments, you return to the same choices.

Intending to do things differently is not enough. It is worth discovering what brings you back to the same reactions.

Echilibrarea rădăcinilor — beyond what appears on the surface

We begin with the specific situation hurting you now. We explore what you experienced, what you understood about yourself and how these perceptions influence your choices.

We work with balancing questions to view experiences from different perspectives. Conscious creation is present from the very beginning of the process.

The aim is to understand your reactions, clarify your choices and begin consciously building what you want in your life.

The questions are mine. The answers are yours.

In individual work, we begin with the situation concerning you now. We explore what you perceived, what you understood about yourself and where you encountered the same conclusions before.

Sometimes an answer opens several directions. I do not automatically stop at the first explanation or decide what your experiences mean for you.

We also explore opposite perspectives through questions from the universal laws, then check how you see the experience and what changes in your reactions and choices. Conscious creation is present during the work.

Between meetings, we follow what you notice in daily life. If the difficulty persists, we continue exploring without assuming a single root.

DO YOU RECOGNISE YOURSELF HERE?

Is choosing for yourself without feeling guilty difficult?

You say yes when you want to say no. You postpone your own plans so as not to disappoint. And when you do something for yourself, you cannot enjoy it: you think about who might be upset.

What it costs you: tiredness, dissatisfaction and the feeling that your choices are no longer yours.

What you may have learned: that others' needs matter more, that love means sacrificing yourself or that you are responsible for how they feel. See whether these ideas are familiar; we do not assume they are true for every woman.

What we can explore together: the current situation, experiences that mattered to you, associated perceptions and how they are reflected in present choices. We work at your pace, without promises of guaranteed change.
