Maybe you say yes when you want to say no. You turn down help, yet feel hurt when nobody offers it. Or you know what you want, but still look for someone else's approval. We explore what happens, without assuming the cause in advance.

Working with Iulia

Iulia Bobeica is a personal development counsellor and the creator of Echilibrarea rădăcinilor (Root Balancing). Her approach explores perceptions, personal values and recurring patterns through questions and reflection. Systemic constellations are a separate, optional offering.
