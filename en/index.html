✧ TIME FOR YOU. A NEW PERSPECTIVE.

You have tried doing things differently. Why do you end up in the same place again?

What happens hurts and you do not understand why you find yourself in the same situation again. Perhaps you tried changing something, but burdensome reactions and thoughts return. We begin with your experience and explore your inner dialogue, story and perceptions about yourself and life, formed in the past.

The Echilibrarea rădăcinilor method

When you begin seeing experiences and conclusions about yourself differently, other possibilities may open for what you choose to experience from now on. Through Echilibrarea rădăcinilor, we explore your inner dialogue, story and perceptions. Systemic constellations are a distinct way of working used according to context.

DO YOU RECOGNISE THIS SITUATION?

You say you do not need help. But it hurts when nobody offers.

You have had a busy week. You wait for the weekend to rest, but find yourself cooking, washing and tidying again. You asked for help, yet things did not change.

And you think, ‘Who will do it if I do not?’

You asked for help and promised yourself you would take care of yourself too. Yet you end up exhausted again. In our work, we explore what you tell yourself about yourself at these moments and how your story may begin to change.

GET TO KNOW THE APPROACH

Your story deserves to be heard in depth.

The work begins with what concerns you now. Through questions, we explore the situation, what you came to believe about yourself and perspectives you may not have seen. As the story you tell yourself changes, you may begin seeing other possibilities and making choices in keeping with what you want.
