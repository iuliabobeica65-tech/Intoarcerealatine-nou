THE PERSON BEHIND THE MEETINGS

I am Iulia. I like looking more deeply.

Personal development counsellor
Graduate in Economics and Business Administration, specialising in Marketing

✧

I have always been interested in understanding things deeply, looking at a situation from several perspectives and not stopping at the first explanation. This curiosity is also reflected in how I work with people today.

My way of working, Echilibrarea rădăcinilor, began taking shape from a wish to explore the complexity of perceptions, patterns and personal choices. I am interested in what actually happens in a woman's life, what she already tried and what prevents her making the choices she wants.

In Echilibrarea rădăcinilor, clarification questions establish the facts; balancing questions come only from the universal laws. Balancing concerns the observable action, then we check the interpretation.

Systemic constellations are part of what I use according to context to explore polarisation at the root more easily. Conscious creation begins at the start of the process and continues during the balancing of perceptions.

FROM MY STUDY JOURNEY

Conscious creation and ongoing learning

I have studied Joe Dispenza's work for over eleven years. Practising his meditations, I explored conscious creation and awareness of space. In my experience, this attention to space helped me understand polarised perceptions differently and the possibility of no longer remaining caught in a single perspective. This is how I describe what I understood from practice, not a statement about guaranteed results or certification from him.

Study at the ReGeneration school and systemic constellation training with Anca Maftei also contributed to how I see a person as a whole: their inner parts and relationship with what happens outside. For me, integration means not automatically rejecting what I call ‘negative’ or idealising what I call ‘positive’, but exploring the role of each part and perspective. These are influences from my learning journey; Echilibrarea rădăcinilor remains my method, with its own way of working.

HOW I WORK

Support and challenge in balance

I looked for support. And began discovering my own resources too. I looked for encouragement and information suitable for me. I found fragments, but not the real support I felt I needed. I had many fears, including fear of making mistakes.

At one point, I also began exploring the drawbacks of waiting for support from outside. I noticed I sometimes limited my potential, doubted myself and relied on help that might not come.

I understood I can receive others' support and, at the same time, be on my own side.

In our meetings, I listen and ask questions inviting you to look at other perspectives too. The questions open perspectives on your experience, and answers take shape as we explore what matters to you.

WHY I CARE

Understanding deeply and building further.

For me, understanding why someone reacted in a particular way is not enough. I am also interested in what becomes possible for them when they begin seeing things differently.

I like working in depth without losing sight of real life. I am interested not only in a beautiful explanation, but how a new perspective appears in your choices and actions.

In later meetings, we check what you notice differently in daily life, in your reactions, behaviours and choices. If the situation returns, we explore it again. Together, we follow what you notice differently and how this is reflected in your choices.

I like following what lies beyond the first answer.

When a woman describes a situation, I do not assume I already understand what lies behind it. I ask what happened, what she perceived, what she understood about herself and how she reached that conclusion.

Sometimes several directions open from a single perception. Other experiences, needs and conclusions emerge. We explore how they connect and where they appear in the present.

I do not decide which discovered connections matter to you. We continue exploring through questions, including opposite perspectives, without denying what you experienced or imposing conclusions.

An important moment for me is when the person reaches clarity they recognise and notices they no longer waver in the same way. I am also interested in effects in their real life: in reactions, behaviours and choices.
